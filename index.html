How Eco-Points Work

Students and classes earn Eco-Points for real sustainability actions. Points are awarded automatically from EcoNexus sensors + AI Waste Scanner, and manually by teachers for challenges and good behaviour.

Points Structure

Completing weekly Eco Challenge 20–50 AI Coach + Evidence
